About Adam Hamdan

"A lifetime of service built on integrity, family values, and devotion to liberty."

Principles Over Politics

Adam Hamdan, the Constitution Party candidate for President, brings an unwavering commitment to God, his country, and the original intent of the United States Constitution as the forefathers intended. Born and raised in Bacon County, Georgia, Hamdan combines his natural rural community values with his extensive legal knowledge as a practicing attorney to conserve the life, liberty, and pursuit of happiness that the Constitution provides. Guided by his faith, Hamdan offers a vision on defending religious freedom, and the power of the American people.

Following his military service, Hamdan established Hamdan’s Law Firm, where he serves as CEO, overseeing legal operations and advocating for constitutional rights. He later entered politics, serving two years as a Georgia State Representative for the 34th District. In 2024, he ran as the Constitution Party’s vice presidential candidate, gaining experience in national politics.

My commitment is simple: to keep those Constitutional rights FOR the people.
