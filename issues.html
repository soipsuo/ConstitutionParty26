Our 3 Priority Issues

Restoring founding principles to contemporary governance

Issue 1: Defending Constitutional Limits

Government authority must remain strictly within the bounds specified by the Constitution. We advocate for reducing unelected regulatory power, returning power to state and local levels, and preserving individual rights.

Issue 2: Fiscal Discipline & Economic Liberty

Persistent government debt and overspending diminish the purchasing power of every family. We advocate for balanced budgets, lowering tax burdens, and eliminating government waste.

Issue 3: Preserving Individual Liberties

Protecting freedom of speech, religious liberty, private property rights, and parental authority in education are non-negotiable foundations of a free society.
