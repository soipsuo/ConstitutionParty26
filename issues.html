Our 3 Priority Issues

Restoring founding principles to contemporary governance

Issue 1: Americans' Access to Healthcare

We will repeal federal health care law and eliminate the FDA in order to remove federal restrictions on medical treatments, medical devices, pharmaceuticals, allowing individuals to have full access to medical options. The federal government has no Constitutional provision to restrict the freedom of the people to have access to medical care, supplies or treatments.

Issue 2: Preserving Religious Freedom

Protecting the right of private organizations to determine their membership, volunteers, and employment standards based on their oaths and creeds, and repeal any taxes on churches and other religious organizations is our goal. We believe in the First and 10th Amendments which forbid such type of tyranny where the federal government restricted the people’s right to practice their own religion.

Issue 3: Adjusting the Federal Economy

Recommending a reform of the system of Federal taxation. In order to do that, the US will return to the money system set forth in the Constitution, repeal the Federal Reserve Act, abolish the Federal Reserve banks and prohibit fractional reserve banking.
