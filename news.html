Hamdan Proposes Targeted Border Funding After 326 Arrests in San Luaso

BACON COUNTY, Ga. — Constitution Party presidential candidate Adam Hamdan is calling on Congress to include 200 additional immigration case-processing personnel in any new border-security funding package following the arrest of 326 people near San Luaso. His proposal supports enforcement by helping officials verify identities, review immigration records, and prepare cases for lawful decisions within 60 days of initial processing.
